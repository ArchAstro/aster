# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be considerate, welcome different perspectives, give constructive feedback,
and focus disagreement on ideas and behavior. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and publishing
another person's private information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the ArchAstro maintainers through the
contact options on the [ArchAstro GitHub organization](https://github.com/ArchAstro).
Maintainers will review reports in confidence, take proportionate corrective
action, and avoid conflicts of interest when assigning reviewers. Enforcement
may range from a warning to permanent exclusion from project spaces.
