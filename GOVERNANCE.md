# Governance

Aster is maintained by the ArchAstro maintainers.

Routine changes are reviewed through pull requests. Maintainers weigh technical
quality, compatibility, maintenance cost, and community impact and aim for
consensus. When consensus cannot be reached, the maintainers make the final
decision and document material tradeoffs in the issue or pull request.

Maintainers may merge, release, triage, moderate, and manage project
infrastructure. New maintainers are added based on sustained, constructive
contributions and demonstrated judgment. The maintainer team may revise this
process as the project grows, through a reviewed pull request.
